FIYOTA Noir Two-Tone — black glossy dial, gold hands, silver tank, two-tone links
================================================================================
Read from the product photo: polished stainless steel tank case with rounded
corners; GOLD fluted crown on the right; deep BLACK glossy dial with no printed
pattern; four slim GOLD baton indices at 12/3/6/9 only (no numerals, no date);
GOLD dauphine hands plus a thin gold sweep seconds with a counterweight tail;
white-silver script wordmark FIYOTA in the upper half; five fine "beads of rice"
link rows with the MIDDLE rows gold and the TWO OUTER ROWS ON EACH SIDE polished
silver; the cylindrical T-bar lugs are silver with a gold centre link.
