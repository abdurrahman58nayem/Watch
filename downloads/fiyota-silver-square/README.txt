FIYOTA Silver Square — silver rectangular case, square stone markers
====================================================================
Read from the product photo: polished stainless steel rectangular case with a
stepped bezel and fine ribbed coining on the sides; knurled silver crown at 3;
silvery-white satin dial with NO printed texture; four small SQUARE faceted
stone-set markers at 12/3/6/9 only (no numerals, no date); silver dauphine hands
plus a thin silver sweep seconds with a counterweight tail; dark grey script
wordmark FIYOTA in the upper half; five-row polished steel block-link bracelet
with a wide flat solid end link.
