FIYOTA Crystal Two-Tone — round gold case, crystal markers, TWO-TONE bracelet
=============================================================================

MIDDLE rows warm gold tone and the OUTER rows on both sides bright polished
silver steel; the cylindrical T-bar end lugs are silver steel; the round case,
bezel and crown are gold tone. The gold rows look pale/whitish in the phone
photos because of the overhead light — they are gold in the metal.
